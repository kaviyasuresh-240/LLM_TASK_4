Importance of Text Embeddings in Semantic Search and LLM Applications

Introduction
Text embeddings are numerical representations of sentences that capture semantic meaning. Unlike keyword matching, embeddings allow machines to understand context and relationships between concepts.

Project Summary
- Collected 20 diverse sentences.
- Generated embeddings using all-MiniLM-L6-v2.
- Computed cosine similarity across sentence pairs.
- Identified the top five most similar pairs.
- Visualized results with a heatmap and clustering.
- Saved embeddings and similarity scores in CSV format.

Observations
- Sentences about technology, including AI, Python, and blockchain, clustered together.
- Environmental topics, such as the Amazon rainforest, climate change, and UN warnings, showed high similarity.
- Unrelated domains, such as the human brain and oil prices, had low similarity scores.

Applications
- Semantic Search: Improves retrieval by meaning, not just keywords.
- Clustering and Topic Modeling: Groups related content automatically.
- LLMs: Embeddings help large language models retrieve context, maintain coherence, and answer questions more accurately.

Conclusion
Embeddings bridge human meaning and machine understanding. This project demonstrates their role in modern NLP systems, making search, recommendation, and conversational AI more intelligent and context-aware.
